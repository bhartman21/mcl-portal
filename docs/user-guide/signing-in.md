# Signing in with an email link

> **Not live yet.** The sign-in screens are built, but the portal is not yet connected to its live sign-in service. Until it is, pressing **Send Sign-In Link** shows this message:
> "Firebase is not configured. Use Demo Sign-In below for local preview."
> This guide shows how sign-in will work once it is turned on.

You do not need a password. The portal emails you a link. You click the link to sign in.

## Sign in

1. Open the portal home page.
2. Press **Member Sign In**. You will find it on the home page and at the top right of every page.
3. The **Member Sign In** page opens.
4. In the **Email Address** box, type your email address.
5. Press **Send Sign-In Link**.
6. A green message appears: "Sign-in link sent to *your email*. Check your inbox and click the link on this device."
7. Open your email. Find the message with the sign-in link.
8. Click the link. Use the **same computer, tablet, or phone** where you typed your email.
9. The portal opens and you are signed in.

**Tip:** If you do not see the email, check your spam or junk folder.

**Important:** Open the link on the same device and the same web browser you used in step 4. If you open it somewhere else, you may see: "Please enter your email address to complete sign-in." If that happens, go back to step 1 on the device you want to use.

## After you sign in

- Your name or email shows at the top right of the page.
- Under your name you see your role, such as **MCL Member**.
- The home page now shows **Open VAVS Tracker** and **Open Ad Book Studio**.

## If your account is waiting for approval

Sometimes the Department turns on approval for new members. Then you will see **Registration Pending Approval**.

- This means a Department Administrator must approve you first.
- You do not need to do anything. Wait for an administrator to approve you.
- You can press **Sign Out** and come back later.

## If your account is suspended

If you see **Account Suspended**, please contact your Department of Florida Administrator.

## Sign out

1. Look at the top right of the page.
2. Press **Sign Out**.
3. You return to the home page.

## Other things you may see

- **Continue with Google.** This button is on the sign-in page. The Department's chosen way to sign in is the email link. Like the email link, this button will not work until the portal is connected to its sign-in service.
- **Local Preview Sign-In.** This box only shows while the portal is not connected to its sign-in service. It is for testing. It lets a tester pick a pretend role: **MCL Member**, **Detachment VAVS Rep**, **Ad Book Approver**, or **Department Admin**. A **Local Preview** badge also shows at the top of the page.
