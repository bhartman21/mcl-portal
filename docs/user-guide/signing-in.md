# Signing in with an email link

> **Not live yet.** The sign-in screens are built and tested, but the portal's Firebase project is not set up yet. Until it is, pressing **Send Sign-In Link** shows:
> "Firebase is not configured. Use Local Demo Sign-In below."
> This guide shows how sign-in will work once the portal is live.

You do not need a password. The portal emails you a link. You click the link to sign in.

## Sign in

1. Open the portal home page.
2. Press **Member Sign In**. You will find it on the home page and at the top right of every page.
3. The **Member Sign In** page opens.
4. In the **Email Address** box, type your email address.
5. Press **Send Sign-In Link**.
6. A green message appears: "Sign-in link sent to *your email*. Check your inbox and open the link (on another device you will be asked to confirm your email)."
7. Open your email. Find the message with the sign-in link.
8. Click the link.
9. The portal opens and you are signed in.

**Tip:** If you do not see the email, check your spam or junk folder.

## If the portal asks you to confirm your email

This happens if you open the link on a different computer, phone, or browser than the one you used in step 4. It can also happen if the email address saved on that device does not match the link.

1. You will see a blue message. For example: "It looks like you opened your sign-in link on a different device or browser. Please re-enter your email address to finish signing in."
2. In the **Confirm Email Address** box, type the same email address the link was sent to.
3. Press **Finish Sign-In**.

## After you sign in

- Your name or email shows at the top right of the page.
- Under your name you see your role, such as **MCL Member**.
- The home page now shows **Open VAVS Tracker** and **Open Ad Book Studio**.

## If your account is waiting for approval

Sometimes the Department turns on approval for new members. Then you will see **Registration Pending Approval**.

- This means a Department Administrator must approve you first.
- You do not need to do anything. Wait for an administrator to approve you.
- You can press **Sign Out** and come back later.

## If your account is suspended

If you see **Account Suspended**, please contact your Department of Florida Administrator.

## Sign out

1. Look at the top right of the page.
2. Press **Sign Out**.
3. You return to the home page.

## Local Demo Mode (testing only)

Until the portal is live, a **Local Demo Mode** badge shows at the top of every page. The sign-in page also shows a **Local Demo Mode (no Firebase)** box. Testers can pick a practice role there: **MCL Member**, **Detachment VAVS Rep**, **Ad Book Approver**, or **Department Admin**. Nothing is saved to a server in this mode.

Signing in with Google is not offered. The Department chose the email link.
