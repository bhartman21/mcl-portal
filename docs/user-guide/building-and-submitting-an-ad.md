# Building and submitting an ad

Ad Book Studio lets you make an ad for a Department program book. You then send it to an approver.

> **Please note: your ads are saved only on this computer for now.**
> Ad Book Studio keeps your ads in the web browser you are using. It does not send them to the Department yet.
> - If you change computers or browsers, you will not see your ads.
> - An approver on a different computer cannot see your ad yet.
> - Clearing your browser history may erase your ads.
>
> Saving ads to the Department's shared system is planned.

You must be signed in to use Ad Book Studio.

## Open Ad Book Studio

1. Sign in. See [Signing in with an email link](./signing-in.md).
2. Press **Ad Book Studio** at the top of the page. Or press **Open Ad Book Studio** on the home page.
3. The **Convention & Event Ad Books** page opens.
4. Your ads are listed under **My Ads**.

## Start a new ad

1. Press **Create New Ad**. (If you have no ads yet, you can also press **Create Your First Ad**.)
2. The **Create New Ad** page opens.

## Fill in your ad

1. **Ad / Project Title** — type a name for your ad. This is required. Example: "Detachment #54 Convention Greeting".
2. **Sponsor / Detachment Name (Optional)** — type who the ad is from, if you like.
3. **Ad Size** — pick a size from the list. The usual sizes are:
   - **Full Page** — 7.5″ × 10″
   - **Half Page (Horizontal)** — 7.5″ × 4.875″
   - **Quarter Page** — 3.625″ × 4.875″
   - **Business Card** — 3.5″ × 2″
4. **Upload Artwork** — press the box and choose your file.
   - You can use a picture (JPEG, PNG, or WebP), a PDF, or a Word file (.docx).
   - The file must be 10 MB or smaller.
   - Old Word files (.doc) do not work. In Word, save the file as .docx or .pdf first.
   - A PDF or Word file is turned into a picture for you. You will see "Converting document to print-ready PNG..." while it works.
   - If your PDF has more than one page, use **PDF Page** to pick the page you want.

## Adjust how your ad looks

The **Live Print Frame** on the right shows your ad at the right shape.

1. **Image Fit**:
   - **Cover (Fill Frame)** fills the whole space. Some edges may be cut off.
   - **Contain (Full Image)** shows your whole picture. There may be blank space around it.
2. Move the picture with the **Horizontal** and **Vertical** sliders.
3. With **Cover (Fill Frame)**, you can also drag the picture with your mouse. Look for **Drag image to reposition**.

## Save a copy for yourself (optional)

After you add artwork, two buttons appear:

- **Save as PNG** saves a picture file of your ad.
- **Save as PDF** saves a PDF of your ad at the chosen size.

## Save or submit

- Press **Save Draft** to keep working later. You will see "Draft saved."
- Press **Submit for Approval** when the ad is ready.
  - The ad must have a title and artwork.
  - You return to the Ad Book Studio page.

## Check on your ad

Each ad under **My Ads** shows a status:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **draft** | You are still working on it. Only you can see it. |
| **submitted** | It is waiting for an approver. |
| **approved** | It was approved. It can be placed in a program book. |
| **rejected** | The approver asked for changes. See [When an ad is rejected or locked](./rejected-or-locked-ads.md). |

## Buttons on your ad

- **Edit** — change a draft or rejected ad.
- **Submit** — send a draft or rejected ad for approval, right from the list.
- Trash can (**Delete draft**) — delete a draft. **Be careful:** it is deleted right away. There is no "Are you sure?" question.
- **View** — look at a submitted or approved ad.
- **Recall to Draft** — take back a submitted or approved ad so you can change it. You will need to submit it again. If the ad was in a program book, it is taken out of that book (unless the book is locked or printed).

## Payment

The portal does not take payment for ads. Payment is handled offline. Ask your Department staff how to pay.
