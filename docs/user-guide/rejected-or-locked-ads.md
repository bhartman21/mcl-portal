# When an ad is rejected or locked

## When an ad is rejected

An approver may ask you to fix something in your ad. This is called a rejection. It is normal. You can fix the ad and send it again.

### How you will know

1. Open **Ad Book Studio**.
2. Under **My Ads**, the ad shows the status **rejected**.
3. A red box shows **Revision Requested:** and the approver's notes.

### Fix and send it again

1. Press **Edit** on the rejected ad.
2. At the top, a yellow box shows **Approver Notes:** with what to fix.
3. Make the changes. For example, upload a sharper picture.
4. Press **Save Draft**. The ad goes back to **draft**.
5. Press **Submit for Approval** when you are ready.

You can also press **Submit** on the ad in the **My Ads** list.

**Remember:** each ad can be submitted up to **3 times**. The ad shows how many you have used, for example **Submission 2 of 3**.

## When an ad is locked

If an ad is rejected on its **3rd** submission, it is locked.

### How you will know

Under **My Ads**, a yellow box on the ad says:

**Submission limit reached.** An administrator must reset this ad before it can be edited or submitted again.

The ad still shows the status **rejected**. The **Edit** and **Submit** buttons are gone. Only **View** is left.

### What to do

1. Read the approver's notes under **Revision Requested:**.
2. Contact a Department, National, or Site Administrator. Ask them to reset the lock on your ad. Tell them the ad's title.
3. When they reset it, the ad goes back to **draft** with **Submission 0 of 3**.
4. Fix the ad and submit it again. You get up to 3 more submissions.

If you open a locked ad with **View** and try to save it, you will see: "Submission limit reached. An administrator must reset this ad before it can be edited."

A locked ad can't be deleted.

### Not the same thing: a locked program book

Approvers can mark a whole program book as **Locked**. That stops new ads from being added to that book. It does not lock your ad. If you have questions about a book, ask your ad book approver.
