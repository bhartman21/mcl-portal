# Logging VAVS hours

> **Planned — not available yet.**
>
> The VAVS Service Tracker is not built yet. You cannot log hours in the portal today.
>
> The **VAVS Tracker** page exists, but it is only a placeholder. Its **New Entry (Phase 2)** button does not work yet.
>
> Please keep using your detachment's current way of reporting VAVS hours.

## What is planned

From the project plan (`PROJECTPLAN.md`, Phase 2, and `features/VAVS.md`):

- Members will log their own volunteer hours, miles, tolls, cash, and in-kind donations.
- Hours will be grouped by detachment and by half-year reporting period.
- Detachment and Department VAVS reps will be able to review and correct entries. Every change by a rep will be recorded.
- Reports will be available to print and to download as a spreadsheet file (CSV).

Step-by-step instructions will be added here when the tracker is built.
