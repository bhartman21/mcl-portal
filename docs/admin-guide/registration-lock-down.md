# Registration lock-down and new members

For: Department Admins and National Admins.

You choose what happens when someone signs in to the portal for the first time.

## The two choices

- **Open (Auto-Approve)** — new people can use the portal right away as an **MCL Member**. This is the normal setting.
- **Approval Required (Lock-Down)** — new people must wait. They see **Registration Pending Approval** until an administrator approves them.

Changing this setting only affects **new** sign-ins. People who already use the portal are not changed.

## Change the setting

1. Press **Admin** at the top of the page.
2. Press **Registration Settings**.
3. The **Portal Registration Settings** page opens.
4. The current choice shows a badge that says **Active**.
5. To lock down, press **Enable Lock-Down Mode**.
6. To open again, press **Use Open Registration**.
7. A green message confirms the change, for a few seconds.

## Approve people who are waiting

1. Press **Admin**, then **Users & Roles**.
2. If anyone is waiting, you will see **Pending Approval Queue** at the top, with a count.
3. Each row shows the person's name, email, and default detachment.
4. Press **Approve** to let them in as an active member.
5. Press **Suspend** to turn them away.

There is no "Are you sure?" question. The change happens right away.

You can also give roles to a new person. See [Roles: who can do what](./roles.md).

## Good to know

- A pending person cannot use Ad Book Studio or the VAVS Tracker.
- The portal does not email people when they are approved. You may want to tell them yourself.
