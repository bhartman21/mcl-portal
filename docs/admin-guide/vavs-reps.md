# VAVS reps

For: Detachment VAVS Reps and Department VAVS Reps.

> **Planned — not available yet.**
>
> The VAVS Service Tracker is not built. Reps cannot view, correct, or report hours in the portal today.
>
> An administrator can already give you the **Detachment VAVS Rep** or **Department VAVS Rep** role. See [Roles: who can do what](./roles.md). The role does not unlock any tools yet.

## What is planned

From `PROJECTPLAN.md` (Phase 2), `features/VAVS.md`, and `ROLES_AND_PERMISSIONS.md`:

- **Detachment VAVS Reps** will see and correct hours for their own detachment.
- **Department VAVS Reps** will see and correct hours for the whole Department.
- Every change a rep makes will be recorded: who, when, and what changed. Reps will be able to see this history.
- Reps will print reports and download spreadsheet files (CSV) for a reporting period.
- Reporting periods will be half-years: Jan 1 – Jun 30 and Jul 1 – Dec 31.

Step-by-step instructions will be added here when the tracker is built.
