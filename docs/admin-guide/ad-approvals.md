# Approving and rejecting ads

For: Ad Book Approvers, Department Admins, National Admins, and Site Administrators.

> **Please note: ads are saved only on one computer for now.**
> Ad Book Studio keeps ads in the web browser where they were made. The **Approval Queue** only shows ads submitted in **your** browser on **your** computer. You cannot yet see ads that members submit from their own computers.
>
> Shared saving is **planned — not available yet**.

## Open the Approval Queue

1. Press **Ad Book** at the top of the page. On a phone or narrow screen, press the menu button (☰) at the top right first.
2. Press **Approval Queue**. A red number shows how many ads are waiting.
3. The **Ad Approval Queue** page opens.

If nothing is waiting, you will see **Queue Clear**.

## Look at an ad

Each waiting ad shows:

- The ad title
- Who made it ("By ...") and the sponsor name, if given
- The ad size
- A picture of the ad

## Approve an ad

1. Look over the ad.
2. Press **Approve**.
3. You will see "Ad approved and ready for book placement."

There is no "Are you sure?" question. The ad is approved right away.

Next, you can place the ad in a program book. See [Event books and ad sizes](./event-books-and-ad-sizes.md).

## Reject an ad (ask for changes)

1. In **Rejection Notes (required if rejecting, min 5 chars)**, type what needs to change. Be clear and kind. Example: "Logo resolution too low for full-page print."
2. Press **Reject with Notes**.
3. You will see "Ad returned to member with revision notes."

Your notes must be at least 5 characters. If they are too short, you will see "Rejection notes must be at least 5 characters."

The member will see your notes. They can fix the ad and send it again.

**The 3-submission limit:** each ad can be submitted up to 3 times. If you reject an ad on its 3rd submission, it is **locked**. The member can't edit or resubmit it until an administrator resets it. See [Resetting a locked ad](./resetting-a-locked-ad.md).

## Your own ads

Nobody reviews their own ad.

- **Ad Book Approvers:** on your own ad you will see "This is your own ad. A different approver must review it." There are no buttons.
- **Department Admins, National Admins, and Site Administrators** may approve their own ad, but only with a reason. You can't reject your own ad. Withdraw it instead.
  1. A yellow box explains the rule.
  2. Type in **Reason for approving your own ad (required)**.
  3. Press **Approve My Own Ad**.
  4. You will see "Your ad was self-approved. The reason was logged and the other admins were notified."

## Undo a review (administrators only)

Department Admins, National Admins, and Site Administrators see a **Reviewed Ads** section lower on the queue page. It lists approved and rejected ads. Use it if an ad was approved or rejected by mistake.

1. Find the ad under **Reviewed Ads**. A **Self-approved** badge shows if an admin approved their own ad.
2. Type in **Reason for undoing (required)**.
3. Press **Undo Review**.
4. You will see "Review undone. The ad is back in the queue as submitted."

The ad goes back to **submitted**. It does not use up one of the member's submissions. If the ad was locked, undoing the rejection also unlocks it. If the ad was in an open program book, it is taken out of that book. The undo is recorded in the [audit log](./notifications-and-audit-log.md).

If an approved ad is already in a **Locked** or **Printed** book, you will see "Placed in locked book "*book title*". Remove it from the book before undoing." Reopen the book first. See [Event books and ad sizes](./event-books-and-ad-sizes.md).

## Good to know

- The portal does not track payment. An approved ad is **not** the same as a paid ad. Payment is handled offline.
- Approvers and administrators can't delete members' ads. Only the member can delete, and only while the ad is a draft.
