# Approving and rejecting ads

For: Ad Book Approvers, Department Admins, and National Admins.

> **Please note: ads are saved only on one computer for now.**
> Ad Book Studio keeps ads in the web browser where they were made. The **Approval Queue** only shows ads submitted in **your** browser on **your** computer. You cannot yet see ads that members submit from their own computers.
>
> Shared saving is planned.

## Open the Approval Queue

1. Press **Ad Book Studio** at the top of the page.
2. Press **Approval Queue**. A red number shows how many ads are waiting.
3. The **Ad Approval Queue** page opens.

If nothing is waiting, you will see **Queue Clear**.

## Look at an ad

Each waiting ad shows:

- The ad title
- Who made it ("By ...") and the sponsor name, if given
- The ad size
- A picture of the ad

## Approve an ad

1. Look over the ad.
2. Press **Approve**.
3. You will see "Ad approved and ready for book placement."

There is no "Are you sure?" question. The ad is approved right away.

Next, you can place the ad in a program book. See [Event books and ad sizes](./event-books-and-ad-sizes.md).

## Reject an ad (ask for changes)

1. In **Rejection Notes (required if rejecting, min 5 chars)**, type what needs to change. Be clear and kind. Example: "Logo resolution too low for full-page print."
2. Press **Reject with Notes**.
3. You will see "Ad returned to member with revision notes."

Your notes must be at least 5 characters. If they are too short, you will see "Rejection notes must be at least 5 characters."

The member will see your notes. They can fix the ad and send it again.

## Good to know

- Right now, a member can resubmit a rejected ad as many times as they like. The 3-submission limit is **planned — not available yet**. See [Resetting a locked ad](./resetting-a-locked-ad.md).
- The portal does not stop you from approving an ad you made yourself.
- The portal does not track payment. An approved ad is **not** the same as a paid ad. Payment is handled offline.
